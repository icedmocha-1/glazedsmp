How to join

Three steps and you’re in.

  1. 1

    Open Minecraft Java

    Launch the game on version 1.21.11. Bedrock and console editions can’t connect.

  2. 2

    Add the server

    Go to Multiplayer, choose Add Server, and paste glazedsmpp.joinmc.world as the address.

  3. 3

    Join and say hi

    Select Glazed SMP and hit Join Server. Then jump into Discord to meet everyone.

Server rules

Rules

Keep Glazed SMP fun and fair for everyone.

  • Be respectful

    Treat every player and staff member kindly. No harassment, hate or personal attacks.

  • Griefing and stealing are allowed

    Players can take from and break each other’s things, so protect what matters to you.

  • No cheating

    Hacked clients, x-ray and exploits are not allowed. Play fair.

  • Keep chat friendly

    No spam, advertising or unsafe links in game or in Discord.

  • Build responsibly

    Avoid anything that makes the server lag, and keep builds tidy.

  • Listen to staff

    Follow staff instructions. If you disagree, talk to us in Discord.

These are the starter rules. Staff can change them any time.

Questions

FAQ

Quick answers about joining and playing.

How do I join?

Open Minecraft Java Edition, choose Multiplayer, add a server and use the address glazedsmpp.joinmc.world. The version is 1.21.11.

Can I join on Bedrock or console?

No. Glazed SMP is a Java Edition server, so Bedrock and console editions cannot connect.

Where do I find events?

The Events page lists upcoming events in your own timezone. They are also announced in our Discord.

How do I get help?

Join our Discord and ask in the help channels. Staff and other players are happy to help.

How do I report a player?

Message a staff member in Discord with the player’s name and what happened.
